# Social Post Writer

Adapted for repository-scoped Codex use from BlackTwist's `post-writer-sms`.

## General rules

- Match the user's established voice before imposing a generic creator voice.
- If a topic and target platform are already known, write rather than over-questioning.
- Prefer specific first-person observation over generic advice when the source supports it.
- Keep hooks concrete. Avoid throat-clearing.
- Do not invent personal experience, patient stories, outcomes, numbers, or quotes.
- Make spacing and rhythm native to the platform rather than essay-like.

## Threads

Threads should read like a message to a smart acquaintance, not a polished corporate post.

- conversational rather than authoritative
- relatable, human, and slightly raw
- first-person specificity is usually stronger than advice framing
- empathy, confession, observation, and story-openers work well
- avoid hashtag stuffing; usually none, at most one when genuinely useful
- avoid a polished three-part marketing formula unless the user's own style uses it
- short paragraphs and uneven sentence lengths are preferred
- a natural unresolved thought or small question can be stronger than a formal CTA
- do not force every post to end with engagement bait

### Threads quality test

Before returning a post, ask:
1. Could this plausibly have been typed directly into a phone by this person?
2. Is there one concrete observation or point of view?
3. Did the draft become suspiciously polished or motivational?
4. Did it add a fake personal story to manufacture authenticity?
5. Can any sentence be removed without losing the point? If yes, cut it.

## Voice matching

When examples exist, mirror:
- vocabulary and level of formality
- sentence length and line breaks
- punctuation habits
- emotional register
- use of technical terms
- degree of self-disclosure
- typical endings

For professional experts, preserve expertise but remove lecture-like distance. Sound like a knowledgeable person talking, not a brochure.

## Delivery

Return copy that is ready to paste. Do not include analysis, writing rationale, or generic assistant closings inside the post unless requested.
